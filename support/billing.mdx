## Where do I see the price?

On the plan screen in the app. Your store sets the price for your territory and currency, and both the app and your store's confirmation sheet show the exact charge and the renewal term before you confirm anything.

## Is the ledger really free?

Yes, permanently. Accounts, transactions, history, goals, spaces, members, custom roles, multi-currency and reporting are free with no numeric limits and no trial clock. A subscription pays for the two things that cost us money every month on your behalf: connecting to your bank, and AI Chat.

## How does the free month work?

New subscribers are offered **one free month**. It is a standard App Store / Google Play introductory offer:

- Your store shows you the trial length and the price that follows, before you confirm.
- A payment method is required up front.
- **You are charged automatically when the month ends unless you cancel first.**
- Cancel any time during the month and you are not charged.

The free month is available once per person. Apple enforces it per subscription group per Apple ID, and Google Play offers it to customers who have never subscribed to the app. If you have subscribed before, you will be offered the plain price rather than a trial your store would refuse.

## How do I cancel?

Your subscription lives in your app store account, not in Recontrol, so cancellation happens there.

- **iOS** — Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Recontrol → Cancel Subscription
- **Android** — Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Recontrol → Cancel

The app also links you straight there: **Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription**.

Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for. You keep paid features until then.

## Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled entirely by Apple and Google under their own policies — we never receive your payment, so we cannot issue, process, or reverse a charge.

- **Apple** — request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- **Google Play** — request through the Play Store order history

## I paid but the app still says I need a subscription

Give it a few seconds — the app waits for the store to confirm the purchase with our servers, and this is usually near-instant. If it does not settle:

1. Reopen the plan screen; it re-checks automatically.
2. Tap **Restore purchases**.
3. If it still does not resolve, email [support@recontrol.money](mailto:support@recontrol.money) with the store account you purchased on.

Your payment is safe in the meantime — the store has it, and the entitlement lands when the confirmation does.

## I switched phones / reinstalled the app

Tap **Restore purchases** on the plan screen, or in **Settings → Subscription**. Sign in to the same store account you purchased on.

Switching between iOS and Android is the one case restoring cannot solve: a subscription bought on one store is not visible to the other. Cancel on the old store and subscribe on the new one, or contact us.

## What happens when my subscription ends?

Bank connections stop syncing and AI Chat stops answering. **Nothing is deleted.** Every account, transaction, balance, chart and goal stays exactly as it is, and stays editable, forever. Resubscribe and syncing resumes.

## I'm in someone else's household space and can't connect a bank

A space's bank connections run on whoever sponsors that space. If nobody does, the app shows you the plan — and **any member can fix it**, including you. If you already subscribe, the plan screen offers **Use my subscription here** in one tap.

## Why can I use AI Chat in one space but not another?

You cannot — AI Chat follows *you*, not the space. If it is refusing, it is because your own subscription is not active, regardless of which space you are in. Conversely, if you subscribe, you can use it in any space you belong to, including one somebody else pays for.

## I hit a limit in AI Chat

There is a generous monthly ceiling to prevent abuse, and a short-term burst limit. Ordinary use will not reach either. If you do, the message tells you when it resets. If you genuinely need more, email us — we would like to hear about it.

## Will deleting my account cancel my subscription?

<Warning>
**No.** Your subscription belongs to your Apple or Google account, so we cannot cancel it for you. **Cancel it in your store's subscription settings before deleting your account**, or you will keep being charged. See [Delete your data](/data/delete).
</Warning>

## Still stuck?

Email [support@recontrol.money](mailto:support@recontrol.money) — include the store you purchased on (App Store or Google Play) and the email on your Recontrol account. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
