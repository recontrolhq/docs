## Is Recontrol free?

Yes. Recontrol is currently free to download and use. If paid features are introduced in the future, you will be given at least 30 days' notice and will never be charged unless you explicitly opt in.

## Does Recontrol connect to my bank?

Manual entry is always available and is the default. We also offer **optional** connections:

- **Banks (US & Canada)** via **Plaid** — import balances and transactions automatically.
- **Investments (India)** via **Zerodha** (Kite Connect) — import your equity and mutual-fund holdings.

These are entirely opt-in. When you connect an account, you authenticate directly with the provider — **Recontrol never sees or stores your bank or brokerage login credentials**. You can disconnect at any time, which revokes and deletes the stored access token. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for exactly what is imported.

## Is my financial data private?

Yes. Your data is stored encrypted in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1), and processed by our API server in the United States while you use the app. We do not sell your data and do not run advertising. The app uses one analytics tool, **PostHog**, which records only pseudonymous usage events — never your balances, transactions, name, or email — and you can turn it off in Settings. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for the full picture.

## How do I create a shared space for my household?

Go to **Settings** in the app and tap **Create Space**. Give the space a name, then use **Invite Members** to add people by their email address or phone number. Invited members can accept immediately if they have an account, or when they sign up.

## Can I use Recontrol in multiple currencies?

Yes. You can set a preferred display currency for each space, and Recontrol converts balances into that currency using exchange rates so your totals and net worth are shown in a single currency.

## What happens to my data if I delete my account?

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All data associated with your account — including your profile, all financial accounts, all transactions, all goals, all data imported from connected institutions, all stored integration tokens (which are also revoked with the provider), all space memberships, and all settings — is deleted from our servers. We do not retain backups. Once deletion is complete, your data cannot be recovered by you or by us under any circumstances.

See [Delete your data](/data/delete) for full details and step-by-step instructions.

## I am the only admin of a shared space. Can I still delete my account?

Before deleting your account, you must either transfer admin ownership to another member or delete the space entirely. The app will prompt you through this process during the account deletion flow.

## I forgot my password. How do I log in?

On the login screen, tap **Forgot password** and enter your email address. You will receive a password reset link. If you signed up with **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple**, use that option on the login screen — no password is required.
