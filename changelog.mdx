## AI Chat, subscriptions & a move to Virginia

_July 2026 — v1.3_

**AI Chat** — new
- Ask about your money in plain language, by typing or by voice, from the **Chat** button on Home
- **Recontrol computes every figure; the model only narrates it.** It is instructed to state numbers verbatim from what we computed, never to calculate its own, and to say it does not have a figure rather than guess
- **Read-only** — it cannot create, edit, delete, or move anything
- Honest caveats lead the answer: a connection that stopped syncing, money not yet categorised, and totals that could not be fully currency-converted are all flagged rather than hidden
- Voice mode with editable dictation — a spoken question is never sent without your confirmation — plus copy, share, and opt-in read-aloud on a settled answer
- **No conversation history is stored on our servers.** The transcript lives on your device for the session only. We keep the text of your question; the answer is never stored. See [AI and your data](/data/ai)

**Plans & billing** — new
- The ledger stays **free, permanently** — unlimited accounts, transactions, history, spaces, members and custom roles, with no numeric limits
- A subscription unlocks **connected bank and broker accounts** and **AI Chat**. Auto-categorisation and reports & digests are planned and not yet available
- Monthly and annual plans, purchased through the App Store or Google Play. **One free month** for new subscribers, once per person, enforced by the stores
- **Sponsorship** — any member of a shared space can put their own subscription behind it to pay for its bank connections; several people can sponsor the same space, and withdrawing removes nobody and deletes nothing
- AI Chat runs on **your own** subscription in any space you belong to; bank connections run on whoever sponsors the space
- If a subscription lapses, syncing and chat stop and **nothing else changes** — your books stay fully readable and editable, forever
- Restore purchases, manage and cancel through your app store. **Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription** — the app now warns you and links you to your store's settings

**Account deletion**
- Deletion is **never blocked** by a shared space. A space where you are the only member is deleted with you; a space with other members survives, and if you were its only administrator the longest-standing remaining member is promoted automatically. You no longer have to transfer or delete a space first
- Connected integrations are revoked at the provider before anything is deleted

**Infrastructure & privacy**
- **The database moved from AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India) to AWS us-east-1 (Northern Virginia, United States)**, putting it in the same country as the API that reads it. Sessions, sign-ins and installed apps carried across with no action needed. See [Privacy Policy §8](/legal/privacy#8-data-storage-location-and-international-data-transfers)
- New observability stack — OpenTelemetry traces, metrics and logs through a collector we run ourselves to **Grafana Cloud (US)**, with sensitive fields stripped at the source under a deny-by-default allowlist
- **Crash and error reporting** through PostHog, covered by the same analytics opt-out. Error messages and runtime values are removed before sending; only the error type and code locations are kept
- New processors disclosed: **Anthropic** (AI Chat), **RevenueCat** (subscriptions), **Grafana Labs** (monitoring)
- Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Information Security Policy, Data Retention and Access Controls policies all updated — see the notes at the top of each

## Connected accounts, notifications & more

_June 2026 — v1.1_

**Connected accounts (optional)** — now available
- Connect banks in the **United States and Canada** via **Plaid** to import balances and transactions automatically (up to two years of history on first connect), kept fresh on a schedule and via secure webhooks
- Connect your **Zerodha** account (India) via Kite Connect to import equity and mutual-fund holdings
- Bank and broker login credentials are never seen or stored by Recontrol; access tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM)
- Connected accounts can be disconnected at any time, which revokes and deletes the stored token

**Notifications**
- Opt-in push notifications for connection and account activity, with per-type controls

**Privacy & analytics**
- Added **PostHog** product analytics (US cloud) to help us improve the app — pseudonymous, never your financial data, and **opt-out** in Settings
- Account deletion now runs server-side and sends a confirmation email; sign-in supports email & password, Google, and Apple

**Currency**
- A new Space's reporting currency is defaulted from your device's region (signup no longer asks for a currency)

## v1.0 — Initial release

_March 2026_

Recontrol launches on iOS and Android.

### Core features

**Accounts**
- Create financial accounts across four types: asset, liability, expense, and income
- Set opening balances at account creation
- View per-account balance history

**Transactions**
- Record double-entry transactions between any two accounts
- Set transaction date, amount, and optional description
- Back-date transactions to correct historical balances
- Edit and delete transactions with automatic balance correction

**Spaces**
- Personal space created automatically on signup
- Create shared spaces for households and groups
- Invite members by email address or phone number — invitations wait for recipients who have not yet signed up

**Access control**
- Two default roles per space: admin (full access) and member (full access to accounts and transactions; read-only elsewhere)
- Create custom roles with any combination of permissions across 5 resources and 4 actions
- Role changes take effect immediately for all current holders

**Insights**
- Balance history per account, updated on every transaction
- Aggregate queries: sum, count, average, min, max across any filtered set of accounts
- Date-range filtering

**Authentication**
- Email and password sign-in
- Sign in with Apple

**Account management**
- Account deletion from Settings — permanently and irreversibly deletes all data with no backups
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Service available in-app and at recontrol.money
