## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Recontrol free?">
    **The ledger is free, permanently** — unlimited accounts, transactions, history, goals, spaces, members and custom roles, with no numeric limits and no trial clock.

    Two features carry an ongoing third-party cost and require a **paid subscription**: **connected bank and broker accounts**, and **AI Chat**. New subscribers get one free month, and the price is shown in the app before you confirm. If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted — syncing and chat stop, and your ledger stays readable and editable forever. See [Plans & billing](/features/plans).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is AI Chat, and is it safe?">
    AI Chat lets you ask about your own money in plain language. It is **read-only** — it cannot create, edit, delete, or move anything.

    It also does no arithmetic: Recontrol computes every figure first, and the model may only state numbers that appear in what we computed. If it does not have a figure, it says so rather than inventing one.

    Your question and a snapshot of computed totals go to **Anthropic** (Claude) to be phrased into an answer. Individual transactions, your name and your email are never sent. **We store your question; we never store the answer.** See [AI and your data](/data/ai).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Recontrol connect to my bank?">
    Manual entry is always available and is the default. We also offer **optional** connections: banks in the **US and Canada** via **Plaid** to import balances and transactions, and **Zerodha** (India, via Kite Connect) to import your investment holdings. You authenticate directly with the provider — Recontrol never sees or stores your bank or brokerage login credentials — and you can disconnect at any time. Connections are part of the paid plan. See [Connected accounts](/features/connections).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my financial data private?">
    Yes. Your data is stored encrypted in **Northern Virginia, United States (AWS us-east-1)**, and our API server is in the United States too. *(Our database was in Mumbai, India until 29 July 2026 — see the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy#8-data-storage-location-and-international-data-transfers).)*

    We do not sell your data and do not run advertising. The app uses one analytics tool, **PostHog**, which records pseudonymous usage events and stripped crash reports — never your balances, transactions, name, or email — and you can turn it off in Settings. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for the full picture.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create a shared space for my household?">
    Go to **Settings** in the app and tap **Create Space**. Give the space a name, then use **Invite Members** to add people by their email address or phone number. Invited members can accept immediately if they have an account, or when they sign up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Recontrol in multiple currencies?">
    Yes. You can set a preferred display currency for each space, and Recontrol converts balances into that currency using exchange rates so your totals and net worth are shown in a single currency.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I delete my account?">
    Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All data associated with your account — including your profile, all financial accounts, all transactions, all goals, all data imported from connected institutions, all stored integration tokens (which are also revoked with the provider), your AI Chat questions, our record of your subscription, all space memberships, and all settings — is deleted from our servers. We do not retain backups. Once deletion is complete, your data cannot be recovered by you or by us under any circumstances.

    **Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription** — that belongs to your app store account. Cancel it there first, or you will keep being charged.

    See [Delete your data](/data/delete) for full details and step-by-step instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I am the only admin of a shared space. Can I still delete my account?">
    Yes. Deletion is never blocked, and you do not need to hand the space over first. A space where you are the only member is deleted with your account; a space with other members survives, and if you were its only admin, the longest-standing remaining member is promoted automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?">
    Cancel through your app store — **Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription** in the app links you straight there. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.

    Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies; we never receive your payment, so we cannot issue one. See the [billing FAQ](/support/billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I forgot my password. How do I log in?">
    On the login screen, tap **Forgot password** and enter your email address. You will receive a password reset link. If you signed up with **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple**, use that option on the login screen — no password is required.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
