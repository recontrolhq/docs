Recontrol has one principle behind its pricing:

> **We charge for delegated labour, never for the ledger.**

Everything you do yourself — recording transactions, keeping books, sharing them with your household — is free, and stays free. A subscription pays for the things that cost us money every month on your behalf: talking to your bank, and talking to a language model.

## What is free, forever

The whole double-entry ledger, with no numeric limits of any kind:

- Unlimited accounts, transactions, and history
- Unlimited [spaces](/features/spaces), members, custom roles and permissions
- Multi-currency with live exchange rates
- Net worth, balance history, totals and [insights](/features/insights)
- Manual entry of everything

There is no cap on accounts, no cap on transactions, no cap on people you share with, and no trial clock on any of it. You can also request a copy of your data at any time, on any plan — see [Your rights](/data/rights).

## Plans and the free month

There are two ways to pay — **monthly** or **annually** — for exactly the same thing. Annual is the better value and is preselected.

Before you can subscribe, the plan screen asks you to turn on **AI Answers**. It is off by default, and its caption names Anthropic and lists exactly what AI Chat sends there. We ask because AI Chat is the only feature that hands your figures to a third party, and you should agree to that specifically rather than have it bundled into a purchase. See [AI and your data](/data/ai).

New subscribers get **one free month**, once. It is a standard App Store / Google Play introductory offer: your store shows you the trial length and the price that follows before you confirm, takes your payment method up front, and charges you when the month is up unless you cancel first. Cancel at any point during the month and you are not charged.

The free month is available once per person, and the stores enforce that — on Apple by subscription group, on Google Play to customers who have never subscribed to the app. If you have subscribed before, you will be offered the plain price rather than a trial the store would refuse.

<Note>
**Prices are shown in the app, on the plan screen, in your own currency.** We do not publish them here, because your store sets the local price. The exact charge and renewal term are always stated on screen before you confirm.
</Note>

## What happens if you stop paying

Nothing is deleted, hidden, or arithmetically broken. Ever.

| | Bank sync | AI Chat | Your ledger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** (never subscribed) | — | — | Full: read and write |
| **Active** | ✓ | ✓ | Full |
| **Lapsed** (subscription ended) | Stopped — history intact | — | **Full: read and write, forever** |

If your subscription ends, connected accounts stop refreshing and AI Chat stops answering. Everything already in your books — every account, transaction, balance, chart and goal — stays exactly where it is, stays editable, and stays yours. Resubscribe and syncing resumes; nothing was lost.

This is deliberate. A finance app that holds your own history hostage behind a renewal is one of the things we set out not to build.

## Who pays for what

Recontrol asks two separate questions, and it is worth understanding the difference in a shared household.

**Bank connections belong to the book.** A connection keeps syncing (and costing) long after it is linked, so it is paid for by whoever puts their subscription behind that space — the **sponsor**. Any member of a space may sponsor it, and several people may sponsor the same space.

When you subscribe, **every space where you are the only member is sponsored for you automatically** — nobody else is affected, so there is nothing to ask. A **shared** space is never sponsored on your behalf: putting your money behind a book other people use is your decision, so it takes one explicit tap, from the plan screen or from Settings.

**AI Chat belongs to the person.** It is metered per question against whoever asks, so it runs on **your own** subscription, in any space you belong to. A subscriber invited into someone else's household can use chat there. A non-subscriber cannot use it, even in a space someone else is paying for. Two members of the same space can have two different answers.

Manage this from **Settings → Subscription → Spaces your subscription sponsors**. Switching a space on pays for its bank connections. Switching it off stops that space's connections syncing — it does not remove you from the space and deletes nothing.

<Note>
Sponsoring is always something you do for yourself. No role, and no administrator, can commit someone else's money.
</Note>

## Managing your subscription

Your subscription lives in your **App Store or Google Play account**, not in Recontrol. That is where it renews, where payment methods change, and where it is cancelled.

- **Restore purchases** — on a new device, or after a reinstall, tap **Restore purchases** on the plan screen.
- **Change or cancel** — **Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription** opens your store's own subscription settings. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. We cannot issue them.

<Warning>
**Deleting your Recontrol account does not cancel your subscription.** The subscription belongs to your store account, so we cannot end it for you. Cancel it in your store's subscription settings *before* you delete your account, or you will keep being charged. The app warns you about this during deletion.
</Warning>

## Fair use

There is no cap on how many banks you connect. AI Chat has a generous monthly ceiling that ordinary use will not reach; if you do reach it, the app tells you the date it resets. See [AI Chat](/features/chat#fair-use).

## Relationships

- A subscription belongs to a **person**, not a space.
- A sponsorship links one person's subscription to one [Space](/features/spaces), and disappears if they leave it.
- [Connected accounts](/features/connections) require the space to be sponsored.
- [AI Chat](/features/chat) requires the caller's own subscription.
