Connecting an institution is optional. Manual entry is always available, is the default, and is free forever. A connection simply saves you the typing.

<Note>
Connected accounts are part of the paid plan. A space's bank connections run on whoever sponsors that space — see [Plans & billing](/features/plans#who-pays-for-what).
</Note>

## What you can connect

| Provider | What comes in | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Plaid** — banks and card issuers | Account names, types, the last four digits of the account number, balances and currency, and transaction history — up to **730 days (two years)** on first connect | United States and Canada |
| **Zerodha** (Kite Connect) — broker | Equity and mutual-fund holdings: ISIN, security or fund name, quantity, average price, last price, and profit or loss | India |

One connection covers **every account inside that institution**. Linking a bank with a current account, a savings account and two cards is one connection, not four.

## We never see your login

You authenticate directly with your bank or broker, inside the provider's own flow.

<Warning>
**Recontrol never receives, sees, or stores your banking or brokerage username, password, PIN, or one-time codes.** That exchange happens between you and Plaid or Zerodha.
</Warning>

What we store is an access token that lets us refresh your data. It is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM on top of the database's own encryption, and it is revoked and deleted the moment you disconnect. See [Security](/data/security).

## Keeping data fresh

- **Bank connections (Plaid)** refresh when Plaid notifies us — by secure, cryptographically verified webhook — that your bank has reported new activity. There is no polling: your data updates when there is something to update. Right after you first connect, the app watches for the initial import and fills in balances and transactions as they land.
- **Zerodha** holdings sync automatically on a schedule on trading days. Zerodha's access tokens expire **every day** by design, so you will be asked to re-authorise periodically to keep syncing.

## Disconnecting

Disconnect any connection at any time from the app. When you do, we revoke the token with the provider and delete it from our systems.

Accounts, transactions and holdings already imported stay in your space until you delete them — your charts and history do not develop a hole. Disconnecting only stops further syncing.

## Adding a connection

Adding a connection asks one question first: **is anybody's active subscription sponsoring this space?** If nobody is, you will be shown the plan. Any member of the space can put their own subscription behind it — you do not need to find an administrator or the person who created it.

To limit cost and abuse, there is a short-term limit on how many connection attempts one person can make in an hour. Normal use will not reach it.

## Relationships

- A connection belongs to one [Space](/features/spaces).
- Imported accounts behave exactly like [accounts](/features/accounts) you create by hand.
- Imported transactions behave exactly like [transactions](/concepts/transactions) you record by hand.
- Stale connections are flagged in [AI Chat](/features/chat#honest-caveats), so an answer is never computed silently over data that stopped arriving.
