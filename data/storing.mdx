## Data you provide directly

| Data | Details | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Email address** | Required at sign-up | Account creation and authentication |
| **Display name** | Set during sign-up | Identifying you within shared spaces |
| **Password** | Stored as a one-way hash — we cannot read it | Authentication |
| **Phone number** | Optional | Inviting members to a space by phone |
| **Financial account names and types** | Accounts you create (assets, liabilities, income, expenses) | Core functionality |
| **Transaction data** | Amounts, descriptions, timestamps, from/to accounts | Core functionality |
| **Goals** | Goal names, target amounts, target dates, linked accounts | Goal tracking |
| **Space names** | Names of spaces you create or join | Collaborative workspace management |
| **Reporting currency** | The display currency for each space. Defaulted from your device's region when a space is created, and changeable in-app | Display formatting and conversion |
| **Support messages** | Emails you send to support@recontrol.money | Customer support |

## Data collected automatically

| Data | Details | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Authentication tokens** | JWT access tokens and refresh tokens | Session management |
| **Session identifiers** | Login timestamps and session IDs | Security |
| **Country / region** | A two-letter country code from your device's region setting at sign-up (not GPS or IP geolocation); omitted if unavailable | Establishing your country/region for regional privacy rights and defaults |
| **Device push token** | An Expo push token, device name, and platform — only if you enable notifications | Sending notifications you turn on |
| **Product analytics** | Pseudonymous usage events and device/app metadata via PostHog — never your name, email, or financial data; opt-out in Settings | Improving the app |
| **Basic request metadata** | IP address, user-agent string, request timestamps | Server operation, security monitoring, debugging |
| **Server-side metrics** | API response times, error rates (instrumented with OpenTelemetry) | Infrastructure monitoring — server-side only, not linked to you personally |

The app includes one product-analytics tool, **PostHog** (US cloud). It records pseudonymous usage events to help us improve the app and **never** receives your financial data, name, or email. You can turn it off in **Settings**. We use no advertising SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or cross-app tracking.

## Data from connected institutions (optional)

If you choose to connect an external financial account, we import data from it **on your behalf, with your consent**. These integrations are optional and inactive until you connect an account; manual entry is always available. We **never** receive your banking or brokerage login credentials.

| Integration | What we import | Region |
|---|---|---|
| **Plaid** (banks) | Account names, types, last 4 digits of the account number, balances, currency, and up to 2 years of transactions (amount, date, description, merchant, pending status), plus your institution's name | United States & Canada |
| **Zerodha** (Kite Connect) | Equity and mutual-fund holdings (ISIN, name, quantity, average price, last price, P&L) and your Zerodha user ID, name, and email | India |

We store an access token for each connected integration so we can refresh your data. These tokens are **encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM**, on top of the database's own encryption. You can disconnect at any time, which revokes and deletes the token. See [Security](/data/security).

## Data we do not collect

- Precise or GPS location, and no IP-based geolocation (we do record the coarse country code from your device's region setting at sign-up — see above)
- Device contacts or address book
- Photos or camera access
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA or similar)
- Your bank or brokerage login credentials (authentication is handled by Plaid / Zerodha — we never see them)
- Biometric data (the optional app lock uses your device's on-device biometrics; the data never reaches us)

## Where your data lives

Recontrol's **database is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India) region** (via Nhost). The data you enter, and the data we import on your behalf, is stored at rest in India. Our **application/API server is hosted in the United States** (on Railway), so your data is processed there in transit while you use the app.

**If you are outside India** (including in the US or Canada), your data is stored in India and processed in both India and the United States. We stay accountable for protecting it wherever it is processed. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy#8-data-storage-location-and-international-data-transfers) for the full cross-border disclosure and your regional rights.

**Plaid processing:** if you connect a bank using **Plaid**, your banking data is processed by Plaid on infrastructure in the **United States** before it reaches us in India. This only happens if you choose to use the bank-connection feature.

## Third-party services

| Service | Provider | What they receive | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nhost** | Nhost | Email, password hash, display name, phone number, session tokens, financial data | Authentication, database hosting (PostgreSQL via Hasura) |
| **AWS** (via Nhost) | Amazon | All data stored in the database (encrypted at rest) | Cloud storage in Mumbai, India |
| **Railway** | Railway | Data in transit through our API (processed, not stored) | Hosting our API server (United States) |
| **Plaid** | Plaid Inc. | Bank account and transaction data — only if you connect a bank | Bank-account aggregation (US/Canada) |
| **Zerodha** | Zerodha (Kite Connect) | Investment-holdings data — only if you connect Zerodha | Investment-holdings aggregation (India) |
| **Resend** | Resend | Your email address and email content | Transactional email (US) |
| **Expo** (push) | Expo → Apple/Google | Device push token and notification content — only if you enable notifications | Push-notification delivery (US) |
| **PostHog** | PostHog | Pseudonymous usage events and device metadata — no financial data; only if analytics is on | Product analytics (US cloud) |
| **exchangerate-api.com** | ExchangeRate-API | Currency codes only — no personal data | Exchange rates |
| **Apple / Google** (sign-in) | Apple; Google | Your name and email — only if you use that sign-in | Authentication |

We use no advertising services, cross-app tracking, social-media pixels, or data brokers. Our only analytics tool is PostHog (opt-out, no financial data).

## Local storage on your device

The mobile app uses:

- **Expo Secure Store** — stores authentication tokens using iOS Keychain / Android Keystore. Not accessible to other apps.
- **AsyncStorage** — persists session state locally.

These are used to keep you logged in and to cache data locally. If analytics is enabled, the PostHog SDK also stores a pseudonymous identifier and a small event queue on your device. None of this is used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## How long we keep it

| Data type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and profile data | Until you delete your account |
| Financial data (accounts, transactions, goals) | Until you delete your account |
| Imported data (bank accounts, transactions, holdings) | Until you delete it, disconnect the source, or delete your account |
| Integration access tokens | Until you disconnect or delete your account; Zerodha tokens also expire daily |
| Authentication tokens | Short-lived; expire automatically. Invalidated on logout or deletion. |
| Session data | Duration of the active session |
| Server-side operational metrics | Up to 12 months, then deleted or anonymised. Not linked to individual users. |
| Support communications | Up to 24 months after last communication |

See the [Data Retention and Disposal Policy](/legal/data) for full detail.

## Who can see your data within shared spaces

When you join a shared space, other members of that space can see the financial data within it — accounts and transactions — in accordance with their assigned role. Your display name and email may also be visible to other members of any space you belong to.

Data in one space is never visible to members of a different space.
