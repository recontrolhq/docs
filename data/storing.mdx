## Data you provide directly

| Data | Details | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Email address** | Required at sign-up | Account creation and authentication |
| **Display name** | Set during sign-up | Identifying you within shared spaces |
| **Password** | Stored as a one-way hash — we cannot read it | Authentication |
| **Phone number** | Optional | Inviting members to a space by phone |
| **Financial account names and types** | Accounts you create (assets, liabilities, income, expenses) | Core functionality |
| **Transaction data** | Amounts, descriptions, timestamps, from/to accounts | Core functionality |
| **Goals** | Goal names, target amounts, target dates, linked accounts | Goal tracking |
| **Space names** | Names of spaces you create or join | Collaborative workspace management |
| **Reporting currency** | The display currency for each space. Defaulted from your device's region when a space is created, and changeable in-app | Display formatting and conversion |
| **AI Chat questions** | The text of questions you ask in [AI Chat](/features/chat) — never the answers | Understanding which questions people ask so we can build them into the product |
| **Support messages** | Emails you send to support@recontrol.money | Customer support |

## Data collected automatically

| Data | Details | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Authentication tokens** | JWT access tokens and refresh tokens | Session management |
| **Session identifiers** | Login timestamps and session IDs | Security |
| **Country / region** | A two-letter country code from your device's region setting at sign-up (not GPS or IP geolocation); omitted if unavailable | Establishing your country/region for regional privacy rights and defaults |
| **Device push token** | An Expo push token, device name, and platform — only if you enable notifications | Sending notifications you turn on |
| **Subscription status** | Whether you hold a plan and when it renews or expires, an anonymous customer identifier issued by our billing provider, your monthly AI usage count, and which spaces you have chosen to sponsor | Unlocking paid features and enforcing fair-use limits |
| **Product analytics** | Pseudonymous usage events and device/app metadata via PostHog — never your name, email, or financial data; opt-out in Settings | Improving the app |
| **Crash and error reports** | Uncaught app errors and native crashes via PostHog, with the error *type* and code locations only — messages and runtime values are stripped before sending; covered by the same opt-out as analytics | Fixing bugs |
| **Basic request metadata** | IP address, user-agent string, request timestamps | Server operation, security monitoring, debugging |
| **Server-side telemetry** | Traces, metrics and logs from our API — timings, error rates, route names, and opaque internal identifiers such as your user ID. Never names, amounts, balances, emails, or free text | Infrastructure monitoring and debugging |

**Analytics is limited and optional.** The app includes one product-analytics tool, **PostHog** (US cloud). It records pseudonymous usage events and crash reports to help us improve the app, and **never** receives your financial data, name, or email. You can turn it off in **Settings**, and it is disabled entirely on development builds. We use no advertising SDK and no cross-app tracking.

## AI chat

[AI Chat](/features/chat) sends your question and a snapshot of computed figures to **Anthropic** (Claude) to be narrated back to you. We store your question; we never store the answer, and there is no server-side conversation history.

Exactly what is sent, what is never sent, and what Anthropic does with it is documented separately in [AI and your data](/data/ai).

## Data from connected institutions (optional)

If you choose to connect an external financial account, we import data from it **on your behalf, with your consent**. These integrations are optional and inactive until you connect an account; manual entry is always available. We **never** receive your banking or brokerage login credentials.

| Integration | What we import | Region |
|---|---|---|
| **Plaid** (banks) | Account names, types, last 4 digits of the account number, balances, currency, and up to 2 years of transactions (amount, date, description, merchant, pending status), plus your institution's name | United States & Canada |
| **Zerodha** (Kite Connect) | Equity and mutual-fund holdings (ISIN, name, quantity, average price, last price, P&L) and your Zerodha user ID, name, and email | India |

We store an access token for each connected integration so we can refresh your data. These tokens are **encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM**, on top of the database's own encryption. You can disconnect at any time, which revokes and deletes the token. See [Security](/data/security) and [Connected accounts](/features/connections).

## Subscriptions and payments

<Warning>
**We never see, receive, or store your card or payment details.** Purchases are made entirely inside the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment method belongs to your store account.
</Warning>

Purchases are processed by **Apple** or **Google** and reconciled through **RevenueCat**, our subscription-management provider. What reaches us is only: whether an active plan exists, when it expires, and an opaque customer identifier. No card number, no billing address, no transaction amounts.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your device type and operating system, and the store's own purchase receipt or token. It acts as our processor and stores that data in the United States.

## Data we do not collect

- Precise or GPS location, and no IP-based geolocation (we do record the coarse country code from your device's region setting at sign-up — see above)
- Device contacts or address book
- Photos or camera access
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA or similar)
- Your bank or brokerage login credentials (authentication is handled by Plaid / Zerodha — we never see them)
- Your card or payment details (handled entirely by Apple and Google)
- Audio recordings — voice questions are transcribed by your device's own speech recognition and we receive only the resulting text
- Biometric data (the optional app lock uses your device's on-device biometrics; the data never reaches us)

## Where your data lives

<Note>
**Changed 29 July 2026.** Recontrol's database moved from AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India) to **AWS us-east-1 (Northern Virginia, United States)**. Nothing about what we collect changed — only where it is stored. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy#8-data-storage-location-and-international-data-transfers).
</Note>

Recontrol's **database is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the us-east-1 (Northern Virginia, United States) region**, via Nhost. The data you enter, and the data we import on your behalf, is stored at rest in the United States. Our **application/API server is also hosted in the United States** (on Railway), so your data is processed in the same country it is stored in.

**If you are outside the United States** — including in Canada, India, or elsewhere — your data is stored and processed in the United States. We stay accountable for protecting it wherever it is processed. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy#8-data-storage-location-and-international-data-transfers) for the full cross-border disclosure and your regional rights.

**Plaid processing:** if you connect a bank using **Plaid**, your banking data is processed by Plaid on infrastructure in the **United States** before it reaches us. This only happens if you choose to use the bank-connection feature.

## Local storage on your device

The mobile app uses:

- **Expo Secure Store** — stores authentication tokens using iOS Keychain / Android Keystore. Not accessible to other apps.
- **AsyncStorage** — persists session state and cached data locally.
- **PostHog local storage** — if analytics is enabled, a pseudonymous identifier and a small event queue.
- **RevenueCat local cache** — your subscription status, so the app knows what you have paid for without a network round trip.
- **Your AI Chat transcript** — held in memory for the session only, and gone when the app closes.

None of this is used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## How long we keep it

| Data type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and profile data | Until you delete your account |
| Financial data (accounts, transactions, goals) | Until you delete your account |
| Imported data (bank accounts, transactions, holdings) | Until you delete it, disconnect the source, or delete your account |
| Integration access tokens | Until you disconnect or delete your account; Zerodha tokens also expire daily |
| AI Chat questions | Until you delete your account, or ask us to erase them. Answers are never stored |
| Subscription status and usage counters | Until you delete your account. Your monthly AI usage counter resets on the 1st of each month |
| Authentication tokens | Short-lived; expire automatically. Invalidated on logout or deletion |
| Session data | Duration of the active session |
| Server-side telemetry and operational logs | Up to 12 months, then deleted or anonymised. Not linked to individual user profiles |
| Support communications | Up to 24 months after last communication |

See the [Data Retention and Disposal Policy](/legal/data) for full detail.

## Who can see your data within shared spaces

When you join a shared space, other members of that space can see the financial data within it — accounts and transactions — in accordance with their assigned role. Your display name and email may also be visible to other members of any space you belong to.

Data in one space is never visible to members of a different space.
