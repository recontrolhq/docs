title: "Your rights"
description: "Your rights over the personal data Recontrol holds about you."
---

Under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and applicable Indian law, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion** — request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data
- **Data export** — request a copy of your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time (including by disconnecting a bank or broker integration)
- **Opt out of analytics** — turn off product analytics and error reporting at any time in the app's Settings
- **Erase your AI Chat questions** — ask us to delete the questions you have asked in [AI Chat](/features/chat). We never store the answers
- **Grievance redressal** — raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer
- **Nominate** — nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app account settings, or email our Grievance Officer at [support@recontrol.money](mailto:support@recontrol.money). We will respond within **30 days**.

If you live in the **United States** or **Canada**, you have additional region-specific rights — see [Privacy Policy §10](/legal/privacy#10-your-rights).

<Note>
**A subscription is not personal data we can erase.** If you subscribe, the subscription itself belongs to your Apple or Google account. Deleting your Recontrol account removes our record of it but does not cancel it — see [Delete your data](/data/delete).
</Note>

For step-by-step deletion instructions, see [Delete your data](/data/delete).
