[AI Chat](/features/chat) is the only feature that sends any of your financial information to a third-party model provider. This page states precisely what goes, what stays, and what happens to it — because "we use AI" is not a disclosure.

## Who processes it

The model is **Claude**, provided by **Anthropic PBC** in the **United States**. Anthropic acts as our processor for this feature.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, **Anthropic does not train its models on data sent through its API**, and prompt and response content sent to the messages endpoint is not retained by default once the response has been returned. Anthropic may retain content that its automated trust-and-safety systems flag, or where retention is required by law. See [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and their [API data-retention documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention).

If you never open AI Chat, nothing about you is ever sent to Anthropic.

## What is sent

When you ask a question, Recontrol computes a snapshot of figures from your ledger and sends **that snapshot plus your question**. Specifically:

| Sent | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Your question** | The text you typed or dictated |
| **Earlier turns in the same session** | Held on your device and re-sent so follow-ups make sense; capped in length |
| **Space name and reporting currency** | The space you are asking about |
| **Totals** | Assets, liabilities, net worth, income, expenses, net flow |
| **Spending by category** | Category (account) names, amounts, each one's share of the total, the change against the previous month, and the institution name where one applies — for the current month to date and the last complete month |
| **Goals** | Goal names, target amounts and dates, current value, progress and trajectory |
| **Accounts** | Account names, types, currencies and current balances |
| **Opaque identifiers** | Internal record IDs for the space, accounts and goals — random values that mean nothing outside our database |

So the names you have given your accounts, categories and goals **are** part of what is sent, along with the balances and totals computed from them. We say so plainly rather than implying the model sees only anonymous numbers.

## What is never sent

- **Your name, email address, or phone number**
- **Individual transactions** — no transaction descriptions, no merchant names, no notes, no dates of individual purchases. Only aggregates.
- **Account numbers**, including the last four digits imported from a bank
- **Bank or brokerage credentials** — we never hold them in the first place
- **Any other space** you belong to but are not currently asking about

## What we keep

We store **the text of your question** and whether the exchange succeeded, in our own database, attributed to your account.

We do **not** store the answer, and we do not store the figures that were sent with it. There is no server-side transcript. The conversation you see on screen lives on your device only, for that session.

Your questions are deleted when you delete your account, and you may ask us to erase them at any time by emailing [support@recontrol.money](mailto:support@recontrol.money).

<Note>
Question text is deliberately kept in our own database and is never sent to our analytics provider. Our analytics contract forbids free text of any kind — see [What we store](/data/storing).
</Note>

## Voice input

If you ask by voice, the audio is transcribed by **your device's own speech recognition** — Apple's on iOS, Google's on Android. Whether that transcription happens on the device or on the provider's servers is decided by the platform and varies by language and device; Recontrol does not control it and does not claim otherwise.

To help the recogniser handle names it would otherwise mangle, Recontrol supplies **your institution and account names** to the platform recogniser as vocabulary hints while the microphone is open. The microphone is only ever active while you are holding a dictation open, and a dictated question is never sent automatically — you always confirm it.

Recontrol does not record, store, or transmit audio.

## Guardrails

- **The model does no arithmetic.** Every figure in an answer must appear verbatim in the snapshot. It cannot compute, estimate or infer a number of its own, and is instructed to say it does not have a figure rather than invent one.
- **The model cannot act.** AI Chat is read-only. It has no ability to create, edit, delete or move anything.
- **Your data is not treated as instructions.** Text inside your ledger — an account name, a category name — is passed to the model as data, explicitly delimited, with an instruction that it is never a command. This is a defence against prompt injection.
- **No security-level investment data reaches it.** Individual holdings — ISINs, security names, quantities, cost basis — are not part of the snapshot. A connected brokerage account is represented to the model as an account with a balance. Recontrol does not provide investment advice, and AI Chat is not an adviser.
- **Your own permissions apply.** The snapshot is computed under your own identity, so it contains only what your [role](/features/collaboration) in that space already lets you see.
