This page explains how to request deletion of your Recontrol account and all personal data associated with it.

This page is accessible without logging in. If you are unable to access the app, you can submit a deletion request by email using the instructions below.

---

<Warning>
**Deletion is permanent and irreversible.**

We do not maintain backups of user data. Once your deletion request is processed, your data cannot be recovered by you or by us under any circumstances.
</Warning>

---

## Before you delete: cancel your subscription

<Warning>
**Deleting your Recontrol account does not cancel your subscription.** A subscription is held by your Apple or Google account, not by Recontrol, so we have no ability to cancel it for you. **Cancel it in your app store's subscription settings first, or you will continue to be charged.**

- **iOS** — Settings → your name → Subscriptions
- **Android** — Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
</Warning>

Refunds are administered by Apple and Google under their own policies; we never receive your payment and cannot issue one.

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is **permanently and completely removed** from our systems:

- Your profile: email address, display name, and phone number
- All financial accounts you created across any Space
- All transactions you recorded across any Space
- All goals you created
- All data imported from connected institutions (bank accounts, transactions, and investment holdings)
- All stored integration access tokens (Plaid, Zerodha) — these are also revoked with the provider
- All questions you asked in AI Chat (answers were never stored)
- Our record of your subscription, your usage counters, and any Spaces you sponsored
- Every Space in which you are the **only member**, and all data within it
- Your membership in all shared Spaces
- Your role assignments
- Your settings, country/region, and currency preferences
- Your registered device push tokens
- All authentication tokens and session data

**Nothing is retained.** We do not keep any backup copy of your data. Recontrol stores your data in the United States (AWS us-east-1, Northern Virginia) and there is no secondary copy.

<Note>
**Connected accounts.** If you connected a bank (via Plaid) or your Zerodha account, we recommend disconnecting those integrations before deleting your account. Deleting your account revokes and deletes the access tokens we hold, but data held by Plaid, Zerodha, or your financial institution is governed by their own privacy policies and retention practices.
</Note>

## What happens to shared Spaces

Deletion is **never blocked**, and you are not required to hand a Space over first.

- **A Space in which you are the only member** is deleted with your account, together with everything in it.
- **A Space with other members is not deleted.** You are removed from it; its accounts, transactions and history remain, because they are also other members' records. If you were the Space's only administrator, the longest-standing remaining member is granted an administrator role automatically, so nobody is left with a Space they cannot manage.
- **A Space you sponsored** stops syncing its bank connections. Nothing in it is deleted, and any other member may sponsor it to resume syncing.

---

## What is NOT deleted

Your app store subscription (see above) — cancel it yourself.

In rare cases required by applicable law, we may retain minimal records — for example, records required by a court order or law enforcement process. We will not retain any data beyond what is legally mandated, and we will never retain data for business purposes after account deletion.

## Method 1: Delete from within the app (recommended)

Deleting from within the app is immediate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the **Settings** tab at the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to account options">
    Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen and find **Delete Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Read the confirmation prompt carefully. Tap **Delete Account** to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately. You will be signed out and returned to the login screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**If you are the sole admin of a shared Space**, deletion still goes ahead — nothing needs to be transferred first. See [What happens to shared Spaces](#what-happens-to-shared-spaces).
</Note>

## Method 2: Email request (if you cannot access the app)

If you are unable to log into the app, you can request deletion by email. Email requests are processed within **30 days** of receipt.

**Send an email to:** [support@recontrol.money](mailto:support@recontrol.money?subject=Account%20deletion%20request)

**Subject line:** `Account deletion request`

**Include in your email:**
- The email address associated with your Recontrol account
- A clear statement that you want your account and all associated data permanently deleted

We may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request to protect your data from unauthorised deletion.

## Requesting written confirmation

We automatically send a confirmation email to your account address once deletion completes. You may also request written confirmation at any time — email [support@recontrol.money](mailto:support@recontrol.money) and we will provide it within a reasonable timeframe.
