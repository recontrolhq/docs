## 3. Description of Service

Recontrol is a personal and collaborative finance management application that enables users to:

- **Track financial accounts:** Create and manage asset, liability, income, and expense accounts
- **Record transactions:** Log double-entry financial transactions between accounts with amounts, descriptions, and timestamps
- **Set goals:** Define financial goals and link accounts to track progress
- **View reports:** Compute net worth, totals, and balance history, with multi-currency conversion
- **Collaborate in Spaces:** Create shared workspaces with role-based access control
- **Manage access:** Define roles with granular permissions within collaborative Spaces
- **Connect institutions (optional):** Link external financial institutions to import balances, transactions, and holdings automatically (see Section 5A)
- **Ask AI Chat (optional, paid):** Ask questions about your own recorded figures in plain language (see Section 5B)

The core ledger — accounts, transactions, goals, Spaces, members, roles, multi-currency and reporting — is provided **free of charge and without numeric limits**. Connected institutions and AI Chat require a paid subscription (see Section 7).

The Service is available on iOS and Android. Feature availability may vary by platform and by region.

**The Service is provided for personal financial management purposes only. Recontrol does not provide financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, or any other professional financial services. See Section 14 for further details.**

## 5B. AI Chat

### 5B.1 What it is

AI Chat is an optional feature of the paid plan that lets you ask questions about your own recorded financial figures in plain language, by typing or by dictation. Answers are generated by a third-party large language model provided by **Anthropic PBC**, narrating figures our backend has already computed from your Space.

### 5B.2 It is read-only

AI Chat cannot create, modify, or delete any data, and cannot move, hold, or transfer funds. It answers questions; it does not act.

### 5B.3 Accuracy and your responsibility

<Warning>
**AI-generated output may be incomplete or incorrect. Do not rely on it for any financial, tax, legal, or investment decision.** Always verify figures against your own records and your financial institution's statements.
</Warning>

The Service is designed so that every figure stated in an answer must come from figures our backend computed, rather than from the model's own calculation, and so that incomplete or unsynced data is flagged. **These are engineering safeguards, not guarantees.** Language models can misinterpret a question, omit relevant context, or phrase an answer misleadingly. We do not warrant that any answer is accurate, complete, current, or fit for any purpose, and Sections 12, 13 and 14 apply in full to AI Chat output.

Answers are the Service's output and are provided "as is." You are solely responsible for any decision you take on the basis of them.

### 5B.4 What is sent to the model provider

When you ask a question, your question and a bounded snapshot of computed figures from the relevant Space are sent to Anthropic. Individual transactions, your name, your email address, account numbers and credentials are **not** sent. Full detail — including what Anthropic does with the data — is in [Privacy Policy Section 4A](/legal/privacy#4a-ai-chat). By using AI Chat you consent to that processing.

### 5B.5 Not securities or investment advice

**Nothing produced by AI Chat is financial, investment, tax, or accounting advice, and it must not be relied upon as such.** Recontrol is not an investment adviser and provides no advisory service, whether or not you hold a subscription. Individual investment holdings are not included in the data supplied to the model; a connected brokerage account is represented to it as an account and a balance. Section 14 applies in full to any output of AI Chat.

### 5B.6 Acceptable use of AI Chat

You agree not to use AI Chat to attempt to extract its underlying instructions, to circumvent its safeguards, to generate unlawful content, or to place automated or excessive load on it. AI Chat is subject to fair-use limits (Section 7.7). We may suspend access to AI Chat, without affecting the rest of the Service, where use breaches this Section.

### 5B.7 Voice input

If you dictate a question, transcription is performed by your device's operating system (Apple or Google), whose terms and privacy policies apply to that processing. We receive only the resulting text. A dictated question is never submitted without your confirmation.
