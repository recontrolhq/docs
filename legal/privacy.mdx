## 4. Connected accounts and financial integrations

Recontrol offers **optional** integrations that let you link an external financial institution so your balances, transactions, and holdings import and refresh automatically. You are never required to use them, and manual entry remains fully available.

### 4.1 Plaid (bank connections — United States and Canada)

- We use **Plaid Inc.** ("Plaid") to connect to your bank. Plaid is a regulated financial-data aggregator.
- When you connect a bank, you authenticate directly with Plaid (and, through Plaid, your bank). **Recontrol never sees or stores your bank login credentials.**
- On your behalf, Plaid provides us the data listed in Section 3.2, including up to **730 days (two years)** of transaction history so your account history can be reconstructed.
- Plaid is a **United States-based** service provider, and Plaid processes your banking data on infrastructure located in the United States before it reaches Recontrol. See Section 8 (International data transfers).
- We store a Plaid access token so we can refresh your data — periodically on a schedule, in response to secure webhooks from Plaid, and when you open the App. This token is **encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM**.

**How Plaid uses your data.** When you connect a financial account, Plaid collects information from you and from your financial institution — including identifiers (such as your name and contact details), your account and routing information, account balances, and transaction history — and uses, stores, and processes that information to provide the account-connection service and for the purposes described in Plaid's own privacy policy. Plaid is an independent data controller of the information it collects through this process; its handling of your data is governed by the **[Plaid End User Privacy Policy](https://plaid.com/legal/#end-user-privacy-policy)**, which we encourage you to read.

**Your consent to Plaid.** By connecting a financial account through Plaid, **you grant Recontrol and Plaid the right, power, and authority to access and transmit your personal and financial information from the relevant financial institution, on your behalf**, for the purpose of providing the account-connection feature you have chosen to use. **You also agree that your personal and financial information may be transferred, stored, and processed by Plaid in accordance with the [Plaid End User Privacy Policy](https://plaid.com/legal/#end-user-privacy-policy).** You can withdraw this authorization at any time by disconnecting the integration (Section 4.3), which revokes the token with Plaid and deletes it from our systems.

### 4.2 Zerodha (investment holdings — India)

- We use **Zerodha's Kite Connect API** to import your investment holdings if you connect your Zerodha (demat/trading) account.
- You authenticate directly with Zerodha. **Recontrol never sees your Zerodha password or two-factor codes.**
- On your behalf, Zerodha provides us the holdings data listed in Section 3.2.
- We store a **daily** Zerodha access token, **encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM**. It expires every day, after which you must re-authorise to continue syncing.
- Your use of Zerodha is also governed by [Zerodha's privacy policy](https://zerodha.com/privacy/).

### 4.3 Disconnecting an integration

You can disconnect any integration at any time from within the App. When you disconnect:

- We **revoke** the access token with the provider (Plaid or Zerodha), and
- We **delete** the stored encrypted token from our systems.

Accounts, transactions, and holdings already imported into your Space remain in your Space until you delete them or delete your account. Disconnecting stops all further syncing. We recommend disconnecting all integrations before deleting your account.

## 4A. AI Chat

AI Chat is an **optional** feature that lets you ask questions about your own finances in plain language. It is inactive unless you open it, and it is available only on a paid plan. If you never use it, none of your information is ever sent to the model provider described below.

### 4A.1 Who processes it

The model is **Claude**, provided by **Anthropic PBC**, in the **United States**. Anthropic acts as our processor for this feature.

Before you can subscribe, the subscription screen asks you to turn on **AI Answers**. The switch is off until you turn it on, its caption names Anthropic and lists what is sent, and no data reaches Anthropic unless it is on.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, **Anthropic does not train its models on data sent through its API**, and prompt and response content sent to the messages endpoint is not retained by default once the response has been returned. Anthropic may retain content flagged by its automated trust-and-safety systems, or where retention is required by law. Anthropic's handling of this data is governed by the [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and its published [API data-retention documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention).

### 4A.2 What is sent to the model

We do **not** send your ledger. Before your question reaches the model, our backend computes a bounded snapshot of figures and sends **only that snapshot and your question**:

- your question, and the earlier turns of the same on-device session;
- the Space's name and reporting currency;
- the names and reporting currencies of your other Spaces, where you have more than one — so an answer can say which Space a figure belongs to and name the alternatives when a question is ambiguous. No figures from those Spaces are sent;
- totals: assets, liabilities, net worth, income, expenses, net flow;
- spending by category for the current month to date and the last complete month — category (account) names, amounts, each category's share, the change against the previous month, and the institution name where one applies;
- your goals: names, target amounts and dates, current value, progress and trajectory;
- your accounts: names, types, currencies and current balances;
- opaque internal record identifiers for the Spaces, accounts and goals named above.

The names you have given your accounts, categories and goals therefore form part of what is sent, together with the balances computed from them.

### 4A.3 What is never sent to the model

Your name, email address, or phone number; **individual transactions** (no descriptions, merchant names, notes, or individual dates — aggregates only); account numbers, including the last four digits imported from a bank; any banking or brokerage credential; and any figures from a Space other than the one you are asking about (the names of your other Spaces are included — see Section 4A.2).

### 4A.4 What we store

We store **the text of your question** and the outcome of the exchange, attributed to your account, in our own database. **We do not store the answer, and we do not store the snapshot.** There is no server-side conversation history; the transcript you see exists only on your device, for that session, and is discarded when the App closes.

Your questions are deleted when you delete your account, and you may ask us to erase them at any time (Section 10). Question text is never sent to our analytics provider.

### 4A.5 Voice input

If you ask by voice, the audio is transcribed by **your device's own speech recognition** — Apple's on iOS, Google's on Android. Whether that transcription is performed on the device or on the provider's servers is determined by the platform and varies by language and device; it is not a choice we control. While the microphone is open, the App supplies your **institution and account names** to the platform recogniser as vocabulary hints so it can transcribe them correctly. The microphone is active only while you are dictating, and a dictated question is never sent without your confirmation. Recontrol does not record, store, or transmit audio.

### 4A.6 Limits on what the model can do

AI Chat is **read-only**: it cannot create, edit, or delete data, and it cannot move money. Every figure it states must come verbatim from the computed snapshot — it is instructed not to calculate, estimate, or infer figures of its own, and to state that it does not have a figure rather than produce one. Individual investment holdings are not included in the snapshot. AI Chat does not make any decision about you, and nothing in it constitutes financial, investment, or tax advice (see the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms#14-financial-disclaimers)).

## 4B. Subscriptions and payments

Recontrol offers an optional paid subscription. The core ledger is free and remains free.

<Warning>
**We never receive, see, or store your payment details.** All purchases are made and processed entirely inside the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**, under their own terms and privacy policies. Your payment method belongs to your store account.
</Warning>

### 4B.1 What we receive

We use **RevenueCat, Inc.** to reconcile store purchases into an entitlement. What reaches our systems is limited to: whether an active subscription exists, when it expires, and an opaque customer identifier. We do not receive card numbers, billing addresses, or transaction amounts.

RevenueCat processes, on our behalf and as our processor: an app-generated user identifier, your device type and operating system, and the store's own purchase receipt (Apple) or purchase token (Google). RevenueCat stores this data on Amazon Web Services **in the United States**. See the [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

### 4B.2 Sponsorship in shared Spaces

If you put your subscription behind a Space so its bank connections can sync ("sponsoring"), we record that you have done so. Other members of that Space can see that the Space is sponsored. Sponsoring is always your own act — no role and no administrator can commit your subscription on your behalf, and you can withdraw it at any time.

### 4B.3 Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription

Because your subscription belongs to your Apple or Google account rather than to Recontrol, deleting your Recontrol account removes our record of it but **does not cancel it**. Cancel through your store's subscription settings before deleting your account. See Section 11.

## 7. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, as described in detail in our [Information Security Policy](/legal/security):

- **Encryption in transit:** All data is transmitted over TLS/HTTPS
- **Encryption at rest:** Data stored in our databases is encrypted at rest using AES-256, provided by AWS
- **Application-level token encryption:** Access tokens for connected integrations (Plaid, Zerodha) are additionally encrypted at rest using **AES-256-GCM** before being stored
- **Secure credential storage:** On mobile devices, authentication tokens are stored using platform-native secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via Expo Secure Store)
- **Password hashing:** Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes; we cannot read them
- **JWT authentication:** All API requests are authenticated using signed JSON Web Tokens
- **Role-based access control:** Collaborative Spaces enforce granular permissions (see our [Access Controls Policy](/legal/access))
- **Input validation:** All API requests are validated using Protocol Buffer schema validation (protovalidate)
- **Webhook verification:** Incoming webhooks from Plaid and our subscription provider are verified cryptographically as well as by a shared secret, so a forged or replayed payload is rejected
- **Telemetry redaction:** Monitoring data is filtered at the source by a deny-by-default allowlist before it leaves our systems, so names, amounts, balances, email addresses, tokens and free text are never exported to a monitoring vendor
- **Access controls:** Internal access to user data is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. If you become aware of any security issue, please contact us immediately at support@recontrol.money.

### 7.1 Data breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities in accordance with the laws that apply to them:

- **India:** the Data Protection Board of India and affected users, under the DPDP Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, and applicable requirements under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
- **United States:** affected residents (and, where required, state Attorneys General) under applicable state data-breach-notification laws.
- **Canada:** the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and affected individuals where a breach creates a "real risk of significant harm" (PIPEDA), and the Commission d'accès à l'information for Quebec residents (Law 25); we maintain a record of breaches as required.

See our [Information Security Policy](/legal/security) for our incident-response process.

### 11.2 What gets deleted

<Warning>
**Deletion is permanent and irreversible. We do not maintain backups of user data. Once your deletion request is processed, your data cannot be recovered by you or by us under any circumstances.**
</Warning>

Upon processing your deletion request, we permanently delete:

- Your user account and profile information (email, display name, phone number)
- All financial accounts, transactions, and goals you created
- All data imported from connected institutions (bank accounts, transactions, investment holdings)
- All stored integration access tokens (which are also revoked with the provider before deletion)
- All AI Chat questions attributed to you (answers were never stored)
- Our record of your subscription, your usage counters, and any Space sponsorships
- Your currency preferences, country/region, and other settings
- Your Space memberships and role assignments
- Your registered device push tokens
- Session tokens and authentication data

We send a confirmation email to your account email address once deletion completes. See our [Data Retention and Disposal Policy](/legal/data) for the technical detail.

Pending Space invitations are addressed to an email address or phone number rather than to your account, so an invitation that someone else sent to you is not part of your account record; you can decline it, and it expires on its own terms.

### 11.2A Your subscription is not deleted with your account

<Warning>
**Deleting your Recontrol account does not cancel your subscription.** Your subscription is held by your Apple or Google account, not by us, so we have no ability to end it. Cancel it in your app store's subscription settings **before** deleting your account, or you will continue to be charged. The App displays this warning during deletion and links to your store's settings.
</Warning>

In-app deletions are processed immediately. Email requests are processed within **thirty (30) days** of receipt.

After account deletion, we may retain minimal data only where required by applicable law (for example, records required by a court order or law enforcement process). We will not retain any data beyond what is legally mandated.

### 11.5 Written confirmation

You may request written confirmation that your data has been deleted by emailing support@recontrol.money.

### 11.6 Effect on shared Spaces

Your erasure is never blocked by a shared Space, and you are not required to hand one over first.

- **Spaces where you are the only member** are deleted with your account, together with all records within them.
- **Spaces with other members are not deleted.** Your membership and role assignment are removed; the Space's own accounts, transactions and history remain, because they are the Space's records and other members' personal data, not solely yours. If you were the Space's only administrator, the longest-standing remaining member is automatically granted an administrator role, so the Space is never left unmanageable.
- **Spaces you sponsored** stop syncing their bank connections once your subscription record is removed. Nothing in them is deleted, and any other member may sponsor the Space to resume syncing.

## 14. Cookies and local storage

The Recontrol mobile application does not use cookies. It uses:

- **Expo Secure Store:** To securely store authentication credentials using platform-native secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore)
- **AsyncStorage:** To persist session state and cached data locally on your device
- **PostHog local storage:** If analytics is enabled, the PostHog SDK stores a pseudonymous identifier and a small event queue locally on your device
- **RevenueCat local cache:** Your subscription status, so the App knows what you are entitled to without a network round trip
- **AI Chat session memory:** Your current chat transcript is held in memory only, for the duration of the session, and is discarded when the App closes. It is never written to our servers

None of these are used for advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.
