## 4. Connected accounts and financial integrations

Recontrol offers **optional** integrations that let you link an external financial institution so your balances, transactions, and holdings import and refresh automatically. You are never required to use them, and manual entry remains fully available.

<Note>
These integrations are currently in **limited preview** and not yet generally available. This section describes how they work once enabled.
</Note>

### 4.1 Plaid (bank connections — United States and Canada)

- We use **Plaid Inc.** ("Plaid") to connect to your bank. Plaid is a regulated financial-data aggregator.
- When you connect a bank, you authenticate directly with Plaid (and, through Plaid, your bank). **Recontrol never sees or stores your bank login credentials.**
- On your behalf, Plaid provides us the data listed in Section 3.2, including up to **730 days (two years)** of transaction history so your account history can be reconstructed.
- Plaid is a **United States-based** service provider, and Plaid processes your banking data on infrastructure located in the United States before it reaches Recontrol. See Section 8 (International data transfers).
- We store a Plaid access token so we can refresh your data periodically (approximately every six hours) and when you open the App. This token is **encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM**.
- Your use of Plaid is also governed by [Plaid's End User Privacy Policy](https://plaid.com/legal/#end-user-privacy-policy).

### 4.2 Zerodha (investment holdings — India)

- We use **Zerodha's Kite Connect API** to import your investment holdings if you connect your Zerodha (demat/trading) account.
- You authenticate directly with Zerodha. **Recontrol never sees your Zerodha password or two-factor codes.**
- On your behalf, Zerodha provides us the holdings data listed in Section 3.2.
- We store a **daily** Zerodha access token, **encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM**. It expires every day, after which you must re-authorise to continue syncing.
- Your use of Zerodha is also governed by [Zerodha's privacy policy](https://zerodha.com/privacy/).

### 4.3 Disconnecting an integration

You can disconnect any integration at any time from within the App. When you disconnect:

- We **revoke** the access token with the provider (Plaid or Zerodha), and
- We **delete** the stored encrypted token from our systems.

Accounts, transactions, and holdings already imported into your Space remain in your Space until you delete them or delete your account. Disconnecting stops all further syncing. We recommend disconnecting all integrations before deleting your account.

## 7. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, as described in detail in our [Information Security Policy](/legal/security):

- **Encryption in transit:** All data is transmitted over TLS/HTTPS
- **Encryption at rest:** Data stored in our databases is encrypted at rest using AES-256, provided by AWS
- **Application-level token encryption:** Access tokens for connected integrations (Plaid, Zerodha) are additionally encrypted at rest using **AES-256-GCM** before being stored
- **Secure credential storage:** On mobile devices, authentication tokens are stored using platform-native secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via Expo Secure Store)
- **Password hashing:** Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes; we cannot read them
- **JWT authentication:** All API requests are authenticated using signed JSON Web Tokens
- **Role-based access control:** Collaborative Spaces enforce granular permissions (see our [Access Controls Policy](/legal/access))
- **Input validation:** All API requests are validated using Protocol Buffer schema validation (protovalidate)
- **Access controls:** Internal access to user data is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. If you become aware of any security issue, please contact us immediately at support@recontrol.money.

### 7.1 Data breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities in accordance with the laws that apply to them:

- **India:** the Data Protection Board of India and affected users, under the DPDP Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, and applicable requirements under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
- **United States:** affected residents (and, where required, state Attorneys General) under applicable state data-breach-notification laws.
- **Canada:** the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and affected individuals where a breach creates a "real risk of significant harm" (PIPEDA), and the Commission d'accès à l'information for Quebec residents (Law 25); we maintain a record of breaches as required.

See our [Information Security Policy](/legal/security) for our incident-response process.

If you access the Service from the United States, Canada, or elsewhere outside India, **your personal data is transferred to, stored in, and processed in India.** We want you to understand what this means:

- **Comparable protection.** We protect your data with the technical and organisational safeguards described in Section 7 and our [Information Security Policy](/legal/security), and we remain accountable for that protection regardless of where the data is processed (consistent with the accountability principle under Canada's PIPEDA).
- **Access by authorities.** While your data is in India, it is subject to Indian law and may be accessible to Indian courts, regulators, and law-enforcement or government authorities under lawful process. Likewise, where a sub-processor operates in another country, the data it handles may be subject to that country's laws.
- **Your consent.** By creating an account and using the Service, you consent to the transfer, storage, and processing of your personal data in India as described here.

For residents of **Quebec**, this constitutes a communication of personal information outside Quebec; before relying on it for production use we will conduct the privacy impact / transfer assessment required by Law 25. See Section 10 for region-specific rights.

### 8.2 International processing through Plaid

If you choose to connect a bank using Plaid (available for United States and Canadian institutions), your banking data is processed by **Plaid Inc. in the United States** before it is transmitted to, and stored by, Recontrol in India. ## 11. Account deletion and data erasure

### 11.1 How to delete your account

You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time:

- **In-app:** Navigate to **Settings → Delete Account** within the App
- **Web form:** Visit [https://recontrol.money/data/delete](https://recontrol.money/data/delete)
- **Email:** Send a deletion request to support@recontrol.money

### 11.2 What gets deleted

<Warning>
**Deletion is permanent and irreversible. We do not maintain backups of user data. Once your deletion request is processed, your data cannot be recovered by you or by us under any circumstances.**
</Warning>

Upon processing your deletion request, we permanently delete:

- Your user account and profile information (email, display name, phone number)
- All financial accounts, transactions, and goals you created
- All data imported from connected institutions (bank accounts, transactions, investment holdings)
- All stored integration access tokens (which are also revoked with the provider)
- Your currency preferences and other settings
- Your Space memberships and role assignments
- Session tokens and authentication data

Deletion of your user account cascades across our database, removing the Spaces you own and all records within them. See our [Data Retention and Disposal Policy](/legal/data) for the technical detail.

### 11.3 Processing timeframe

In-app deletions are processed immediately. Email requests are processed within **thirty (30) days** of receipt.

### 11.4 Data that may be retained

After account deletion, we may retain minimal data only where required by applicable Indian law (for example, records required by a court order or law enforcement process). We will not retain any data beyond what is legally mandated.

### 11.5 Written confirmation

You may request written confirmation that your data has been deleted by emailing support@recontrol.money.

### 11.6 Effect on shared Spaces

- Your membership will be removed from all Spaces
- If you are the sole administrator of a Space, you must transfer ownership or delete the Space before account deletion can complete
